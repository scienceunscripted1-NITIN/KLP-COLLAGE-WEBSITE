About KLP College

Kishan Lal Public College (KLP College) in Rewari is one of the oldest and premier co-educational institutions of higher learning in South Haryana.


Established in 1964 in memory of the visionary philanthropist Late Pt. Kishan Lal Sharma, the institution has a long tradition of academic pursuit and educational development.


The college provides opportunities for students to pursue higher education in different fields.
